Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 17, 2026

The JustClips uploader is a private tool used only by the owner of the JustClips channels to upload their own short video clips to their own YouTube channel and TikTok account.

What data it accesses

With the owner's permission through Google sign-in, the tool uses the YouTube Data API to upload videos to the owner's channel and to read that channel's basic statistics (subscriber, view and video counts).

How data is stored

The sign-in token is stored only on the owner's own computer. No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising. The tool does not collect data from anyone other than the channel owner.

Revoking access

Access can be removed at any time from Google Account permissions.

The tool's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. It also follows the YouTube Terms of Service.
